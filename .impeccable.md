## Design Context

### Users

Frontend and platform engineers investigating large TypeScript/React module graphs. They use the HTML report after a CLI analysis to find expensive barrel imports, understand cycles, inspect dependency neighborhoods, and decide where a codemod will have the highest payoff.

### Brand Personality

Energetic, experimental, rebellious. Unstave should feel like an incisive instrument that pulls a codebase apart to expose hidden structural cost, while remaining credible enough for engineering decisions.

### Aesthetic Direction

A dark, high-density "graph teardown" workspace: sharp editorial hierarchy, asymmetric composition, vivid warning accents, and restrained motion tied to analysis state. It should avoid generic observability-dashboard styling, cyan-on-dark AI aesthetics, glass effects, ornamental gradients, and decorative technical clichés.

### Design Principles

1. Make structural problems visually impossible to miss: barrels, cycles, and selected dependency paths receive distinct, meaningful treatment.
2. Keep the graph primary and controls secondary; progressive disclosure should reveal detail without crowding the initial view.
3. Use asymmetric rhythm, hard geometry, and a rare disruptive accent to express the rebellious personality without reducing legibility.
4. Every visual distinction must encode data or interaction state—no decorative metrics, charts, or motion.
5. Keep the artifact portable, accessible, responsive, and entirely self-contained with no network dependency.
